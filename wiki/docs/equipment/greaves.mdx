# Greaves of the Stillborn

The Greaves of the Stillborn are unbreakable leg armor. They resist knockback and forced movement, prevent Slowness and Mining Fatigue, and let the wearer move without the usual slowing effects of cobwebs, honey, and soul sand.

## Abilities

**Anchored** activates automatically after standing still on solid ground for three seconds. While anchored, you take 25% less melee damage, deal 25% more melee damage, and slow nearby enemies by 30% within eight blocks. Moving more than three blocks from the anchor ends the effect.

**Sovereign Ground** activates after holding sneak for one second while standing still on solid ground. For 25 seconds, your attack reach increases by 40%, enemies approaching within 16 blocks are slowed by 50%, and nearby enemies are pushed away every five seconds. Eligible enemies cannot teleport into or out of the affected area. Cooldown: two minutes. Some creatures resist control effects.
