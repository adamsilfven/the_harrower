# Crown of the Hollow

The Crown of the Hollow is unbreakable head armor crafted using a Hollow Core.

It lets the wearer see clearly in darkness and prevents Blindness and Darkness. Keep your eyes on a visible enemy for two seconds and the Crown marks it **Exposed** for 15 seconds. Your attacks against that enemy deal 30% more damage and ignore 40% of its armor.

## Abilities

**Absolute Sight** reveals hostile creatures within 48 blocks. Tap the Crown's assigned key to toggle the outlines. Exposing an enemy by looking at it does not require Absolute Sight to be enabled.

**The Final Witness** marks enemies within 32 blocks after you hold the same key for one second. Marked enemies take 50% more damage from you, and your attacks ignore 50% of their armor. The marks last 20 seconds. Cooldown: 90 seconds.

## Controls and crafting

The default Crown key is **V**, replacing the earlier H binding. You can change it under **Options → Controls → Hollowforged**. Tap to toggle Absolute Sight; hold for The Final Witness.

Crafting-table pattern:

```text
E E E
N C N
O · O
```

E = Echo Shard; N = Netherite Ingot; C = Hollow Core; O = Crying Obsidian; · = empty slot.
