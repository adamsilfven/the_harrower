# Worldshard

Worldshard is an unbreakable, two-handed hammer crafted around a Hollow Core. Carrying it means giving up your off-hand actions.

## Ordinary attacks

Fully recovered melee strikes ignore 50% of the target's armor. They deal 50% more damage against blocking targets or enemies with at least 15 armor, disable blocking shields for five seconds, and knock the target back. Nearby enemies within four blocks take a short shockwave worth 40% of the damage dealt.

## Abilities

**Fracture the Earth** activates when you tap and release the hammer's use control within 0.6 seconds. It sends a shockwave 24 blocks forward and seven blocks wide, dealing 40 base damage, knocking enemies away, and applying **Shattered** for 15 seconds. Cooldown: 30 seconds.

**The Last Argument** requires holding the use control for three seconds, then releasing. It deals 100 base damage within 20 blocks, applies Shattered for 30 seconds, and stuns eligible creatures for four seconds. Cooldown: five minutes. Charging slows movement by 80%. Releasing between 0.6 and three seconds cancels the charge.

**Shattered** halves the target's effective armor. Stuns do not affect players or creatures immune to control effects. Ability damage is subject to enemy defenses.
