# Treads of the Worldbreaker

The Treads of the Worldbreaker are unbreakable boots that increase movement speed by 30%, prevent fall damage, and let you walk on powder snow without sinking.

## Abilities

**Aftershock** activates automatically when you land after falling at least five blocks. Longer falls produce stronger impacts, dealing 10–30 base damage within a radius of four to eight blocks.

**Cataclysm** is activated by pressing sneak and jump while standing on solid ground. It launches you upward. Land within 15 seconds to deliver an empowered slam dealing 60 base damage within 16 blocks, knocking enemies away and stunning eligible creatures for two seconds. Cooldown: 60 seconds. Enemy defenses can reduce the damage.

## Crafting

```text
· · ·
N C N
B · B
```

N = Netherite Ingot; C = Hollow Core; B = Breeze Rod; · = empty slot. Leave the top row empty.
