# Hollow Fragment

*“It weighs nothing. The ground beneath it disagrees.”*

The **Hollow Fragment** is the offering used to summon the Harrower. It is an irregular, dark stone fragment with a pale fracture running through it.

## What it does

The Fragment is not a weapon or an ingredient in the Hollowforged crafting recipes. Its purpose is to activate a correctly constructed **Hollow Cairn**.

Drop the Fragment as an item into either of the two open cells in the Cairn's center. The ritual consumes one Fragment when the summoning succeeds, and the Harrower arrives nearby.

## Obtaining a Fragment

Hollow Fragments are intended to be discovered through exploration and loot rather than crafted at a crafting table.

**Chest-location note:** The public repository snapshot currently does not expose a definitive list of structure chest injections or their final probabilities. Until those exact loot sources are verified, this wiki will not claim that a specific structure or chest has a particular drop rate.

## Can I reuse it?

A successful summoning consumes one Fragment. The **Cairn itself is reusable**, so you can perform another ritual when you find another Fragment.

For the exact construction layout and ritual, see [Summoning the Harrower](./summoning).
