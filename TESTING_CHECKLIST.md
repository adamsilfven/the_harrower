# One consolidated fresh-world gameplay check

Install only the new combined JAR on clients/server. Keep LC Core unchanged. No rendered client test was performed during the build.

1. `/give @s lc_hollowforged:hollow_core 5`. Check the approved heart silhouette, 16×16 hotbar/inventory icon, purple name and tooltip. Hold in each hand in first and third person; drop one and check that its broad face lies flat while normal item bob/spin continues. Check an item frame.
2. Open a crafting table/JEI. Verify all five Hollowforged recipes use the same Core in the existing center slot. Craft each using its original ingredients; confirm exactly one Core is consumed. Check vanilla recipe-book discovery after obtaining a Core. Existing equipment visuals/abilities should be unchanged.
3. Summon and defeat the Harrower in Survival. Confirm the unchanged Cairn/600-soul sequence, aggression/combat, animations, sounds, death and gear behavior. Standalone without LC must work.
4. Wait through the fall and crumble: no items or XP should spawn at death. At about 20.5 seconds (death tick 410), rewards should spill from the remains. Confirm each normal kill yields one Core, 2–4 guaranteed Enchanted Golden Apples, 4–8 Netherite Ingots and 1,000–1,500 XP total. Multiple bonus rows can succeed on one kill. Inspect varied weapons, armor and tools. Check mace Density/Breach, trident Loyalty/Riptide, bow Infinity/Mending, crossbow Piercing/Multishot, boots Depth Strider/Frost Walker and tools Fortune/Silk Touch never coexist in incompatible pairs. Check rare templates/books when that independent roll succeeds. Pick up all drops. No Dragon Egg or Hollowforged equipment should drop.
5. With LC, test 0/1/5 valid owned active Leylines overlapping the death location: bonus probability should be 0/5/25%, with at most one extra Core. Turn a Leyline on/off before death to confirm current-state eligibility. Change it after death to confirm the saved roll does not change. Outside all owned radii, or inside only another player's Leyline, no bonus is expected. One kill cannot empirically prove a probability; inspect multiple kills or the attached boundary/distribution evidence.
6. Test multiplayer kill credit and a tamed attacker. Record which player's owned Leylines qualify. Save/restart or unload/reload during the death sequence: the pending rewards and timer must resume without early, missing, rerolled or duplicate loot. Repeat after payout to confirm no second payout. Check that normal global mob-loot disabling remains effective.
7. Smoke-test the unchanged Hollowforged equipment and previous world items after replacing the JAR. Send any client model/placement issue with the view and hand specified.
