# Harrower combat decisions

The Harrower is a grounded, enormous boss, not a flying creature. Its weight, deliberate attacks and long recovery windows should make it dangerous without turning the fight into random unavoidable damage. Its abilities have distinct telegraphs and no terrain destruction.

The three phases remain intact. The player should be able to read each attack, reposition, dodge and recover. Phase III is more relentless, not cartoonishly fast.

Leyline suppression is based on the summoner's own active Leylines, within 64 blocks. Other players' Leylines do not count. The boss is designed as a difficult optional endgame fight, including for players with powerful equipment.

The approved appearance, animations, sounds and equipment should not be changed casually. This is a gameplay and presentation baseline, not permission to rebalance the boss.
