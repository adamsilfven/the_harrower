# In-game checklist — 0.5.1

1. Let each hand capture you. Check your position from third person: torso held at the fist, head and shoulders exposed, no sitting pose.
2. Time the hold and five crush pulses. Compare with and without White leyline suppression using your normal equipment.
3. Watch the final raise and drop. Confirm a real fall occurs and landing protection behaves normally.
4. Have a teammate rescue you during the hold; verify a safe release. Repeat near uneven ground.

Native isolated tests and source checks are described in TEST_REPORT.md. The exact modpack and your arena remain the final gameplay check.
